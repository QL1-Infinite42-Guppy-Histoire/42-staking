42 Staking

Stake 42 with 42 Chain validators and earn yield. This is direct staking with a validator you choose — not liquid staking (st42). Signing is done with MetaMask.

All products are open source (MIT)

Stake and earn yield

When you stake 42 with a validator, you earn rewards (yield) from the network.

Your stake helps secure the network, and you receive rewards in return. The reward rate is not fixed and varies with network conditions.
